# Night IV: contradiction and change

Two lines are true at once and the first wins. At the sixth bell the wisp writes itself a new first line.

## The girl

THE girl SHALL stay still.

## The wisp

WHEN the girl is near, THE wisp SHALL move toward the girl.
IF the bell is near, THEN THE wisp SHALL move away from the girl.
THE wisp SHALL move toward the girl.

## The sixth bell

WHEN the clock strikes 6, THE wisp SHALL write "WHEN the girl is near, THE wisp SHALL move toward the girl." at line 1.
